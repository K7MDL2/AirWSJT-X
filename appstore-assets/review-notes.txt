AirWSJT-X is a ham (amateur) radio application for aircraft scatter communication on microwave frequencies (1296 MHz and above).

To test the app:
1. Launch AirWSJT-X — the app will show a dark-themed UI with three columns: Stations, Aircraft, and Decodes.
2. Without WSJT-X running, the app will show "Disconnected" status in the top bar — this is expected.
3. The Aircraft panel will populate with nearby aircraft data when an OpenSky Network or ADS-B Exchange account is configured.
4. Full functionality requires WSJT-X (a separate amateur radio application) running on the same machine, and a Hamlib-compatible antenna rotator — this is specialized equipment not available for general testing.

The app requires no sign-in. Aircraft data source credentials are optional and enhance functionality but are not required to launch and explore the interface.

This app is intended for licensed amateur radio operators (ham radio) and uses only standard network protocols (HTTP/HTTPS for aircraft data, UDP for WSJT-X communication on localhost).
